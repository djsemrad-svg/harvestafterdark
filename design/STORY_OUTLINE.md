# Harvest Afterdark: story outline (draft, Nov 21)

A first draft for Daniel to change. Nothing here is built yet except where it says so.
The story events themselves are roadmap item 33.

## The idea in one line

Brightwater survived the Quiet Fall by keeping its head down. You arrive, and the town slowly
learns it can do more than hold on: rebuild, take in strangers, and find out what really happened
at Halvorsen.

## Threads

1. **The Ledger** (built): Ruth's five projects. Each one changes how the town lives.
2. **Halvorsen** (partly built): what the lab was doing, why the medevac crashed, and what the
   serum in the vault really is. Dr. Elias knows more than he says.
3. **The refugees** (item 30): strangers arrive from the coast. The town argues about whether to let
   them in. Your choices tip it.
4. **Dry Creek** (built as a place): the raiders are hungry people, not monsters. One of them has
   family in town.

## Year 1

### Spring: arriving
- Day 1: the run-down homestead. Ruth meets you at the gate (built: guided first week).
- Week 1: meet everyone, plant, build a fence, get a weapon (built).
- Spring 10, Seed and Sprout Fair (built): Marguerite mentions boats on the river "like before".
- Mid spring: the first raid on your farm. Jo shows you how to repair a turret (built: Toolbox).
- Spring 24, Lantern Creek Gathering (built): Wren's lantern is for her brother, lost in the Fall.
- End of spring: Ruth shows you the Ledger and asks for help with the Tavern.

### Summer: holding on
- A radio crackles in Jo's workshop: a voice from the coast asking if anyone is out there.
- Elias asks you to fetch his old notes from Halvorsen (the Clean Room chain starts).
- Militia Trials festival: Cole's volunteers show off. Someone from Dry Creek is caught spying.
- First refugees: a family turns up at the east gate. The town splits on letting them stay.

### Autumn: the truth comes out
- The Halvorsen vault: the serum isn't a cure; it slows the turn. Elias was part of the team.
- Hollow Night festival: the town remembers the Fall. Elias confesses to Ruth (heart event).
- The refugee camp grows. Hal's prices, rations and a theft bring it to a head.
- You choose how the town handles it (a town meeting cutscene).

### Winter: the long dark
- A hard winter raid, the biggest yet. If the Watchtowers and Militia are done, the town holds
  easily; if not, it's a fight at the walls.
- The coast voice on the radio again, closer.
- Long Night festival: everyone, old and new, at one table if you brought them together.

## Year 2 and beyond (later)
- The Coastal Region opens: the voice was real. A fishing village that survived on the water.
- Trade by river. The town becomes a hub.
- Halvorsen's last secret: Lab 6's sub-level (mine depth bands tie in here).
